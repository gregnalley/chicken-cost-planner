🐔 Welcome to the Backyard Chicken Feeding Reference

Finding reliable information about what chickens can and cannot eat can be surprisingly difficult. Many websites answer only a single question, while others provide conflicting advice without explaining why.

Our goal is to build the most complete chicken feeding reference available by combining research-backed information with practical backyard experience. Each food includes safety guidance, edible plant parts, nutritional highlights, preparation recommendations, storage advice, common mistakes, frequently asked questions, and links to our full growing guides whenever available.
